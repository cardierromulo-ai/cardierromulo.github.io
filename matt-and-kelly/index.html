Our Story
A love written slowly, and then all at once.

Matt and Kelly met on a wind-swept October afternoon, introduced by mutual friends over a shared love of old bookshops and bad puns. What began as a friendship deepened over years of long walks, quiet dinners, and every ferry crossing to the Vineyard they could manage.

Last summer, on the same bluff where they had their first Vineyard sunset together, Matt asked Kelly to spend forever with him. She said yes before he finished the sentence. We are overjoyed to begin this next chapter surrounded by the people we love most.
